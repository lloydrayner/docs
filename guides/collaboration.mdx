The app is built for teams — every feature is designed with the assumption that more than one person will be working in the same space at the same time. From granular permission controls to live presence indicators, the collaboration tools give your team a shared source of truth where context lives alongside the work itself, not in a separate inbox or chat thread.

## Sharing and Permissions

You can share individual items — a record, a view, a folder, or an entire workspace — with specific teammates or with your whole team.

To share an item, open it and click the **Share** button in the top-right corner. From the sharing panel, you can invite people by name or email and assign each person one of three access levels:

| Access Level | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **View** | Read records and open attachments; cannot make any changes |
| **Comment** | Everything in View, plus leave comments and @mention teammates |
| **Edit** | Full access to create, update, and delete records within the shared item |

Changes to permissions take effect immediately. You can update or revoke access at any time from the same sharing panel.

## Comments and Discussions

Comments turn every record into a threaded conversation, keeping decisions and context permanently attached to the work they relate to.

To add a comment, open any record and click **Add comment** at the bottom of the detail panel. Comments support rich text formatting, inline code, and file attachments. To start a focused discussion on a specific field value, hover over the field and click the comment icon that appears.

**Resolving threads** keeps the conversation history clean without deleting it. Once a discussion has reached a conclusion, click **Resolve** on the thread. Resolved threads are hidden by default but remain searchable — click **Show resolved** in the comments panel to bring them back.

<Tip>
  Use **@mentions** to route a comment directly to the right person. Type `@` followed by a teammate's name to notify them by email and in-app notification. Be specific in your message — a mention with a clear ask ("@Jordan can you review the pricing before Thursday?") gets faster responses than a vague one.
</Tip>

## Assigning Tasks

Every record in the app can be assigned to one or more team members. Open a record and locate the **Assignee** field — click it to search for a teammate by name and select them. You can assign multiple people to the same record when shared ownership makes sense.

Combine assignments with **due dates** to create lightweight accountability. Set a due date in the corresponding field and the app will surface overdue records in the assignee's home view and trigger any reminder automations you've configured.

To see everything assigned to a specific person, use the **Assignee** filter in any view. Saving this as a named view (e.g., "Jordan's Open Tasks") makes it easy for individuals to check their queue without configuring filters each time.

## Activity Feed

The activity feed is a chronological log of every change made to a record or workspace. Use it to understand what happened, when it happened, and who made the change.

To open the activity feed for a record, click the **Activity** tab in the record detail panel. You'll see a timestamped history of field changes, status updates, comments, assignments, and file uploads.

At the workspace level, navigate to **Settings → Activity** to see a broader log covering all records and members. You can filter the feed by:

- **Person** — view changes made by a specific team member
- **Date range** — focus on a particular time window
- **Action type** — filter to just comments, field changes, or status updates

## Real-Time Presence

When multiple people are viewing or editing the same record, the app shows each person's avatar at the top of the record detail panel. Hover over an avatar to see the person's name and what field they're currently editing.

**Live cursors** in text fields show you exactly where a teammate is typing, so you can avoid writing over each other. The app automatically merges concurrent edits using conflict-free resolution — if two people edit different fields simultaneously, both changes are saved. If two people edit the same field at the same time, the app prompts you to review and reconcile the conflict before saving.

## Guest Access

You can invite people outside your organization to collaborate on specific items without giving them access to your full account. Guests receive a scoped invitation that limits them to the exact workspace, folder, or record you shared.

To invite a guest, open the sharing panel for the item you want to share, enter the guest's email address, choose an access level (View, Comment, or Edit), and click **Send Invite**. The guest receives an email with a link and can access the shared item without creating a full account.

<Note>
  **Guest access is subject to plan limits.** The number of active guests you can invite depends on your subscription plan. Free plans include up to 3 guests, Starter plans up to 10, and Pro and Enterprise plans have unlimited guest seats. To see your current usage, go to **Settings → Team → Guests**.
</Note>
