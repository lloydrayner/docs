## Feature Highlights

**Data Management** gives you a structured, searchable workspace where every record has fields, tags, and history. Use filters and saved views to surface exactly the information you need.

**Notifications** keep you informed without overwhelming you. Tune your preferences per channel — email, in-app bell, or mobile push — and set quiet hours so work doesn't follow you into your off time.

**Integrations** extend the app's capabilities by connecting your existing toolchain. Sync calendars, pipe alerts into Slack channels, or use Zapier to wire up thousands of additional apps with zero code.

**Collaboration** lets the whole team work on the same data simultaneously. Role-based permissions ensure everyone sees only what they need, while comments and @mentions keep conversations in context.

**Automations** remove manual overhead by triggering actions — status changes, notifications, record creation — based on rules you define. No engineering required.

**Import & Export** means your data is never locked in. Migrate from spreadsheets or other tools on day one, and export a full snapshot whenever you need it.
