Billing questions are time-sensitive, and most common issues can be resolved through your account settings without needing to contact support. This page walks you through solutions for failed payments, unexpected charges, invoice access, refund requests, and more. If you do need to escalate, our billing support team typically responds within one business day.

<Note>
  When contacting support about a billing issue, include your **account email address** and the **last 4 digits of your payment card** in your message. This helps our team locate your account and billing records quickly, reducing resolution time significantly.
</Note>

## Payment & Charges

<Accordion title="My payment failed">
  A failed payment is usually caused by an issue with your card details, billing address, or a temporary hold placed by your bank. Work through these steps to resolve it.

  1. **Check your card details.** Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Methods** and verify that the card number, expiry date, and CVC on file are correct. Even a single digit out of place will cause a payment to fail.
  2. **Verify your billing address.** Confirm that the billing address saved in the app matches the address registered with your card issuer exactly, including postcode/ZIP code.
  3. **Contact your bank.** Banks sometimes block unfamiliar or international charges without notifying you. Call the number on the back of your card and ask if a charge from us was declined and why. Ask them to authorise future charges from our payment processor.
  4. **Try a different card.** If you have an alternative card available, add it under **Settings → Billing → Payment Methods** and retry the payment.
  5. **Retry the payment.** Once you've updated your payment details, go to **Settings → Billing** and click **Retry payment** next to the failed invoice.

  <Warning>
    If a payment remains failed for more than **7 days**, your account may be downgraded to the free plan. Resolve the payment issue before this window expires to avoid any disruption to your service.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I was charged the wrong amount">
  An unexpected charge amount is usually explained by a plan change, a proration adjustment, or a usage-based fee. Here's how to investigate.

  **Check for recent plan changes:**
  - Go to **Settings → Billing → Billing History** and review your recent invoices. Each invoice includes a line-item breakdown showing exactly what you were charged for.
  - If you upgraded or downgraded your plan mid-billing period, you will see a **proration** line item. Proration credits you for the unused portion of your previous plan and charges you for the new plan from the change date to the end of the billing period.

  **Understand proration:**
  - When you upgrade, the prorated amount for the new plan is charged immediately.
  - When you downgrade, the credit is applied to your next invoice — you are not charged immediately.
  - Example: if you upgrade from Starter to Pro on day 15 of a 30-day billing cycle, you pay half a month of Pro pricing immediately, minus a half-month credit for Starter.

  **Check for usage-based charges:**
  - Some plans include usage-based components (e.g., additional seats, API calls, or storage). Review your plan limits under **Settings → Billing → Plan** to see if any overage charges apply.

  If after reviewing your invoice you still believe a charge is incorrect, contact support with your account email, the invoice number, and the amount you expected to be charged.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My plan didn't upgrade after payment">
  Occasionally, there can be a short delay between a successful payment and your account reflecting the new plan features.

  1. **Wait 5 minutes and refresh.** Plan activations are usually instant, but can take up to 5 minutes during periods of high load. Hard refresh your browser (`Ctrl + Shift + R` / `Cmd + Shift + R`) after waiting.
  2. **Check your email for a confirmation.** You should receive a receipt and a plan confirmation email. Search your inbox for an email from our billing team. If the email shows the wrong plan, contact support.
  3. **Sign out and back in.** Go to your account menu and select **Sign out**, then sign back in. This refreshes your session and pulls your latest plan entitlements from the server.
  4. **Verify the plan in Settings.** Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan** and confirm which plan is shown as active. If it still shows your old plan after following the steps above, contact support.

  When contacting support, provide your account email and the transaction ID or receipt number from the payment confirmation email so the team can verify the charge and manually apply the plan upgrade if needed.
</Accordion>

## Invoices & Records

<Accordion title="I can't download my invoice">
  All invoices are available for download at any time from your account settings. You don't need to contact support to retrieve them.

  **To download an invoice:**

  1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
  2. Scroll to the **Billing History** section.
  3. Find the invoice you need — invoices are listed in reverse chronological order.
  4. Click the **Download PDF** button next to the invoice.

  **Tips for finance and accounting:**
  - Invoices include your company name, billing address, and VAT/tax ID if you have entered them under **Settings → Billing → Billing Details**.
  - To receive invoices automatically by email, go to **Settings → Billing → Billing Details** and add a **Billing email address** (e.g., your finance team's email). All future invoices are sent there automatically.
  - You can download multiple invoices in one go by selecting them with the checkbox on the left and clicking **Download selected**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I cancelled but was still charged">
  Being charged after cancellation is almost always due to a misunderstanding about when cancellations take effect.

  **How cancellation billing works:**
  - When you cancel your subscription, the cancellation takes effect at the **end of your current billing period** — not immediately.
  - You retain full access to your plan features until that date, and you are charged one final time for the period you have already used.
  - For example, if your billing period runs from the 1st to the 31st of the month and you cancel on the 15th, you still have access until the 31st and will not be charged again after that.

  **To confirm your cancellation date:**
  - Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan**. If your plan is cancelled and pending expiry, you will see a banner stating **"Your plan is active until [date]."**

  **If you were charged after your plan should have expired:**
  - Check whether a new subscription was started unintentionally (e.g., by another admin on the account).
  - Contact support with your account email and the charge date — we will investigate and issue a refund if the charge was made in error.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I need a refund">
  Our refund eligibility depends on the type of charge and when the request is made.

  **Refund policy summary:**
  - **Annual plans:** If you cancel within **14 days** of your annual renewal date, you are eligible for a pro-rated refund for the unused months remaining on your subscription.
  - **Monthly plans:** Monthly charges are generally non-refundable once the billing period has begun. Exceptions are made for duplicate charges or billing errors.
  - **Add-ons and one-time charges:** Reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

  **How to request a refund:**

  1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Billing History**.
  2. Find the charge you want to dispute.
  3. Click **Request refund** next to the relevant invoice, or contact support directly.
  4. Provide your reason for the refund request.

  Refund requests are reviewed within **3 business days**. If approved, the refund is returned to your original payment method and typically appears within **5–10 business days** depending on your bank.

  <Note>
    Refunds are issued to the original payment method only. We cannot issue refunds to a different card or bank account.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

## Payment Methods

<Accordion title="How do I update my billing information?">
  You can update your payment method, billing address, and billing contact details at any time from your account settings.

  **Update your payment method:**

  1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Methods**.
  2. Click **Add payment method** and enter your new card details.
  3. Once added, click **Set as default** next to the new card to make it the primary payment method for future charges.
  4. Optionally, remove the old card by clicking **Remove** next to it.

  **Update your billing address or company details:**

  1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Billing Details**.
  2. Edit your company name, billing address, VAT/tax ID, or billing email address.
  3. Click **Save changes**. Updated details appear on all future invoices.

  <Tip>
    If you need to update the billing details retroactively on a past invoice (e.g., to add a VAT number), contact support with the invoice number and the correct details. We can reissue the invoice with updated information.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>
