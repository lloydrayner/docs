Our support team is here to help you resolve issues quickly and get back to work. You can reach us through live chat, email, or by submitting a support ticket — the right channel depends on your plan and the urgency of your issue. Before reaching out, we recommend checking this documentation and the status page (linked in the app footer) to see if your question is already answered or if there is an active incident.

<Tip>
  Always check the status page before contacting support for errors that affect the whole app. If there is an active incident, our engineering team is already working on it and posting live updates — you don't need to file a ticket.
</Tip>

## What to Include in Your Support Request

Providing the right information upfront helps our team diagnose your issue faster and avoids unnecessary follow-up questions. Before you send your message, make sure to include:

- **Your account email address** — the one you use to sign in, so we can locate your account quickly.
- **What you were trying to do** — describe your goal in plain language, e.g. "I was trying to export a report as a CSV from the Analytics page."
- **Steps to reproduce the issue** — list the exact sequence of actions you took before the error or unexpected behaviour occurred. The more specific, the better.
- **Screenshots or screen recordings** — visual evidence is extremely helpful, especially for UI bugs, error messages, or unexpected layouts. Attach them directly to your email or chat message.
- **Your browser and operating system** — for example, "Chrome 124 on macOS 14 Sonoma" or "Firefox 126 on Windows 11". You can find your browser version in **Help → About** within the browser menu.
- **Any error messages** — copy the exact text of any error message you see, including error codes if shown.

## Support Hours and Response Time SLAs

Response times vary by plan. The times below are targets for **first response** during business hours (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays).

| Plan       | First Response Target | Support Channel                     |
|------------|----------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Free       | 48 business hours    | Email only                           |
| Starter    | 24 business hours    | Email + live chat                    |
| Pro        | 4 business hours     | Email + live chat (priority)         |
| Enterprise | 1 business hour      | Dedicated Slack channel + phone      |

<Note>
  Response time SLAs apply to **business hours** (Monday–Friday, 9 am–6 pm EST). Messages received outside of business hours are queued and responded to on the next business day, except for Enterprise accounts with 24/7 support enabled.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions About Support

<Accordion title="How do I check the status of an existing support ticket?">
  You can view all your open and resolved tickets by signing in to the support portal linked in the app's Help menu. Each ticket shows its current status, the assigned agent, and the full conversation history. You can also reply directly from the portal to add more information or follow up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I escalate an urgent issue?">
  Yes. If your issue is business-critical — such as a complete inability to access the app, data loss, or a security concern — reply to your existing ticket with the word **URGENT** in the subject line, or start a live chat and explain the urgency. Pro and Enterprise customers can also request escalation through their dedicated support channel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I need support in a language other than English?">
  Our core support team operates in English. If you need assistance in another language, we recommend using the community forum where community members in many regions actively provide help. For Enterprise accounts, localised support may be available — ask your account manager for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I report a security vulnerability?">
  Do not report security vulnerabilities through the standard support ticket system or public community forums. Instead, use the dedicated security disclosure form in your account's Help section, or send details to the security contact address listed in our Security Policy page. We follow responsible disclosure practices and aim to acknowledge reports within 24 hours. Please include a clear description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce it, and the potential impact.
</Accordion>
