Your account runs on one of four plan tiers, each designed for a different scale of team and usage. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings → Billing** — no need to contact sales unless you're interested in an Enterprise arrangement. Changes to your plan take effect immediately, and billing is prorated so you only pay for what you use.

## Upgrading Your Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing Settings">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Settings**. Choose **Billing** from the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upgrade Plan">
    Under the **Current Plan** section, click **Upgrade Plan** to open the plan selection screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your New Plan">
    Review the plan cards and click **Select** on the plan you want. Toggle the billing cycle between **Monthly** and **Annual** to compare prices before committing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Payment Details">
    Review your payment method on file. If you need to add or update a card, click **Change Payment Method** at this step. Then review the prorated charge summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Upgrade">
    Click **Confirm Upgrade**. Your new plan activates instantly and any additional seats or features become available immediately. You'll receive a confirmation email with a receipt for the prorated charge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment Methods

The app accepts the following payment methods for all paid plans:

- **Credit and debit cards** — Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover are supported. Your card details are encrypted and stored securely via our payment processor; the app never stores raw card numbers.
- **PayPal** — Link your PayPal account by selecting **Add Payment Method → PayPal** and completing the authorisation flow.
- **Bank transfer (ACH/SEPA)** — Available on annual Pro and Enterprise plans. Contact support to set up bank transfer billing.

To add or update a payment method:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Methods**.
2. Click **Add Payment Method** and enter your new card or account details.
3. Set the new method as your **Default** by clicking the star icon next to it.
4. Optionally remove old methods by clicking **Remove**.

## Invoices & Receipts

All invoices are generated automatically at the start of each billing period and are available immediately in the app.

To download an invoice:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
2. Find the billing period you need and click **Download PDF**.

To add your company's billing details (VAT number, company name, address) to future invoices:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Billing Information**.
2. Fill in your **Company Name**, **Address**, and **Tax / VAT ID** fields.
3. Click **Save**. These details will appear on all invoices going forward.

<Note>
  Billing information updates do **not** retroactively change already-issued invoices. If you need a corrected invoice for a past period, contact support with your account email and the invoice date.
</Note>

## Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time — no cancellation fees apply.

To cancel:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Current Plan**.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription** at the bottom of the page.
3. Select a cancellation reason (optional) and click **Confirm Cancellation**.

After cancellation, your paid features remain active until the end of your current billing period. When that period ends, your account automatically moves to the **Free** plan.

**Data retention after cancellation:** All your data — documents, settings, integrations, and team members — is retained for **90 days** after your account downgrades. During that window you can reactivate your subscription and pick up exactly where you left off. After 90 days on the Free plan, data that exceeds Free plan limits may be archived or removed. You'll receive email warnings 30 days and 7 days before any data is affected.

<Tip>
  If you're considering cancellation due to cost, reach out to us first — we may be able to help. For **Enterprise pricing**, volume discounts, or non-profit rates, [contact our sales team](mailto:sales@example.com) and we'll find a plan that works for your budget.
</Tip>
