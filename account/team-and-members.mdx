The app is built for collaboration — you can invite as many teammates as your plan allows, assign each person a role that matches their responsibilities, and adjust access at any time as your team evolves. All team management takes place under **Settings → Team**.

## Roles and Permissions

Every member of your workspace is assigned one of four roles. Choose the role that reflects the level of access each person needs.

| Role       | What they can do |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Owner**  | Full access to all workspace settings, billing, member management, and data. There can only be one Owner; ownership can be transferred but not shared. |
| **Admin**  | Can invite and remove members, change roles (up to Admin), manage integrations, and access all workspace content. Cannot change billing or transfer ownership. |
| **Member** | Can create, edit, and delete content they own, collaborate on shared resources, and view all workspace content. Cannot manage team settings or billing. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to all workspace content. Cannot create, edit, delete, or comment on any resources. Ideal for stakeholders or external reviewers. |

## Inviting a Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner of the app and select **Settings**. In the left sidebar, choose **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Select the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner of the Team page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Email Address and Select a Role">
    Type the invitee's email address in the **Email** field. Then open the **Role** dropdown and select the appropriate role for this person. You can invite multiple people at once by clicking **Add Another** and entering additional addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. The app immediately dispatches an invitation email to each address you entered. The invited person's status appears as **Pending** in the member list until they accept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Member Accepts the Invitation">
    The invitee opens the email and clicks **Accept Invitation**. If they don't already have an account, they'll be prompted to create one. Once accepted, they appear in your team list with the role you assigned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invitation links expire after **7 days**. If a colleague missed the email or the link expired, return to **Settings → Team**, find their entry under **Pending Invites**, and click **Resend**.
</Note>

## Changing Roles

You can update a member's role at any time as long as you have Admin or Owner access.

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Locate the member whose role you want to change.
3. Click the role badge next to their name to open the role dropdown.
4. Select the new role and confirm the change.

The new permissions take effect immediately — the member does not need to log out and back in.

<Note>
  Admins can assign roles up to and including **Admin**. Only the Owner can promote someone to **Owner** (which simultaneously demotes the current Owner to Admin).
</Note>

## Removing Members

To remove a member from your workspace:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Find the member you want to remove and click the **⋯** (more options) menu to the right of their row.
3. Select **Remove from Workspace**.
4. Confirm the removal in the dialog that appears.

<Warning>
  Removing a member immediately revokes their access to the workspace — they will be signed out and unable to log back in. However, **their data is not deleted**: all content they created (documents, comments, tasks, etc.) remains in the workspace and is still accessible to other members. If you need to reassign their work, do so before removing them.
</Warning>

## Pending Invites

View all outstanding invitations under the **Pending Invites** tab on the Team page. From here you can:

- **Resend** an invite that has expired or was not received.
- **Revoke** an invite to cancel it before the recipient accepts.
- **Change the role** on a pending invite if you assigned the wrong one.

Pending invites count against your plan's seat limit only after they are accepted.
