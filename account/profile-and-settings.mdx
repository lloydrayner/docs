Access your account settings at any time by clicking your avatar or initials in the top-right corner of the app and selecting **Settings** from the dropdown menu. From there you can personalise every aspect of your account — from how your name appears to teammates to how and when you receive notifications.

## Personal Information

Keep your profile details accurate so teammates can identify you and the app can reach you when needed.

- **Display Name** — Enter the name you want shown across the app. Click the **Name** field, type your preferred name, and select **Save Changes**.
- **Email Address** — Your email is used for login and all account communications. To change it, enter a new address in the **Email** field and confirm the change via the verification link sent to your new address.
- **Avatar** — Upload a profile picture by clicking the avatar circle and selecting a PNG, JPG, or GIF file under 5 MB. Remove your current image at any time by selecting **Remove Photo**.
- **Phone Number** — Optionally add a phone number for SMS notifications or account recovery purposes.

<Note>
  Some personal information fields — such as your display name or email — may be locked or pre-filled by your organization administrator. Contact your admin if you need to update a field that appears greyed out.
</Note>

## Preferences

Tailor the app's behaviour to match your working style and location.

- **Timezone** — Select your local timezone from the dropdown so that dates, timestamps, and scheduled notifications display correctly for you.
- **Language** — Choose your preferred interface language. Changes take effect immediately without a page reload.
- **Date Format** — Switch between regional date formats (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY vs DD/MM/YYYY) to match your locale.
- **Theme** — Toggle between **Light** and **Dark** mode, or select **System Default** to automatically follow your operating system's appearance setting.

## Notification Settings

Control exactly which updates the app sends you and through which channels.

### Email Digests

Choose how frequently you receive email summaries of activity in your account. Options include **Real-time**, **Daily Digest**, **Weekly Digest**, or **Never**. Select your preferred cadence from the **Email Notifications** dropdown and click **Save**.

### In-App Alerts

In-app notifications appear in the bell icon in the top navigation bar. Use the toggles under **In-App Alerts** to enable or disable notifications for specific event types, such as mentions, task assignments, or status changes.

### Mobile Push Notifications

If you have the mobile app installed, enable push notifications by toggling on **Mobile Push** for each event category. You can also manage push permissions directly from your device's system settings.

## Password & Authentication

Keeping your login credentials strong and up to date protects your account and your team's data.

- **Change Password** — Enter your current password, then type and confirm your new password. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Click **Update Password** to save.
- **Two-Factor Authentication** — For an additional layer of security, set up 2FA by navigating to **Settings → Security**. See the [Security guide](/account/security) for full setup instructions.

<Note>
  If your organization enforces single sign-on (SSO), your password and authentication method may be managed entirely by your identity provider. In that case, the password section will not be available in your settings.
</Note>
