---

This guide walks you through everything you need to get up and running with the app as quickly as possible. From creating your account to completing your very first task, you'll be fully set up in just a few minutes — no prior experience required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to the app's sign-up page and enter your name, email address, and a secure password. Once you submit the form, check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link inside it. Verifying your email activates your account and ensures you can receive important notifications going forward.

    <Note>
      If the verification email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. You can also request a new verification email from the login page.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Profile">
    After logging in for the first time, you'll be prompted to complete your profile. Add your full name, upload a profile photo, and select your timezone so that dates, times, and scheduled notifications display correctly for your location. A complete profile also helps your teammates identify you when you collaborate on shared work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Head to **Settings → Team** and enter the email addresses of the colleagues you'd like to work with. Each person will receive an invitation email with a link to join your workspace. You can assign roles — such as Admin, Member, or Viewer — at the time of invitation, and adjust permissions at any time from the same settings page.

    <Tip>
      You can invite multiple teammates at once by entering their email addresses separated by commas in the invitation field.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Dashboard">
    Once your workspace is set up, take a few minutes to familiarise yourself with the dashboard. The left-hand navigation gives you access to all major sections of the app. The main panel displays your current activity, recent items, and any tasks or notifications that need your attention. The top bar provides quick access to search, your profile, and account settings.

    <Info>
      You can customise which widgets and sections appear on your dashboard by clicking **Customise** in the top-right corner of the dashboard view.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your First Task">
    To create your first task, click the **+ New** button in the top navigation bar and select **Task** from the dropdown menu. Give your task a descriptive title, set a due date, and optionally assign it to a team member. Click **Save** to add it to your workspace. Your new task will appear immediately in the dashboard and in the relevant project or list view.

    <Warning>
      Tasks are visible to all members of your workspace by default. If you're working on something confidential, use the **Visibility** setting when creating or editing a task to restrict access to specific team members.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  New accounts start on a free trial that gives you full access to all features for 14 days — no credit card required. At the end of your trial, you can choose a paid plan that suits your team's size and needs, or continue on the free tier with reduced limits. Visit the [Billing & Plans](/account/billing-and-plans) page to compare options.
</Note>
