Creating your account is the first step toward getting started with the app. The process takes just a few minutes — you'll sign up with your work email, verify your identity, and configure a few basic preferences before you're ready to go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Sign-Up Page">
    Open your browser and navigate to the app's sign-up URL. On the homepage, click the **Sign Up** button in the top-right corner. If you already have an account, click **Log In** instead to return to your existing workspace.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Details">
    Fill in the registration form with the following information:

    - **Full name** — enter your first and last name as you'd like them to appear in your account.
    - **Work email address** — use your professional email for billing, notifications, and team collaboration.
    - **Password** — create a password that meets the minimum requirements:
      - At least **8 characters** in length
      - At least **one uppercase letter**
      - At least **one number**

    Once you've filled in all fields, click **Create Account** to continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    After submitting the form, the app sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open your inbox and click the **Verify Email Address** link inside the message. The link expires after 24 hours.

    If you don't see the email within a few minutes:

    1. Check your **spam or junk** folder.
    2. Add the sender address to your contacts list and try again.
    3. Return to the sign-up confirmation screen and click **Resend verification email**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    Once your email is verified, you're prompted to complete your profile. Provide the following details to personalise your experience:

    - **Display name** — the name other team members will see.
    - **Avatar** — upload a profile photo (JPEG or PNG, max 5 MB).
    - **Timezone** — select your local timezone so that dates, deadlines, and notifications display correctly.
    - **Language preference** — choose your preferred interface language from the dropdown menu.

    Click **Save and Continue** when you're done. You can update all of these settings later from your profile page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a Plan">
    Select the plan that best fits your needs. Every new account starts with a **14-day free trial** that gives you full access to all features — no credit card required.

    After the trial period, you can choose from a range of paid plans based on your team size and feature requirements. For a full breakdown of plan features and pricing, see the [Billing and Plans](/account/billing-and-plans) page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can also sign up using **Google Sign-In** or your organisation's **Single Sign-On (SSO)** provider. Click **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with SSO** on the sign-up page and follow the prompts. SSO availability depends on your organisation's identity provider configuration.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive the verification email">
    If the verification email hasn't arrived after a few minutes, try the following steps:

    1. Check your **spam, junk, or promotions** folder — automated emails sometimes get filtered.
    2. Wait **at least 5 minutes**, as delivery delays can occasionally occur.
    3. Make sure you entered the **correct email address** during sign-up.
    4. Return to the sign-up confirmation screen and click **Resend verification email** to trigger a new message.

    If you continue to experience issues, contact [support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) with your registered email address and our team will help you verify your account manually.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my email address later?">
    Yes — you can update your email address at any time from your account settings. Navigate to **Profile → Account Settings → Email Address**, enter your new email, and click **Save Changes**. The app will send a confirmation link to your new address before the change takes effect. Your old email remains active until you confirm the new one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. Every new account automatically begins a **14-day free trial** with full access to all features — no credit card required. At the end of the trial period, you'll be prompted to choose a paid plan to continue. If you don't upgrade, your account moves to the free tier with limited features. You can upgrade at any time during or after the trial from the **Billing** section of your account settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
