The dashboard is your central hub for everything in the app. From here you can access all of your workspaces, monitor activity, manage settings, and jump directly into any feature. Understanding the layout helps you work faster and get the most out of the tools available to you.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sidebar">
    The sidebar runs along the left edge of the screen and serves as your primary navigation menu. It gives you instant access to every major section of the app.

    **What you'll find in the sidebar:**

    - **Home** — return to your main dashboard overview at any time.
    - **Workspaces** — switch between or create new workspaces for different projects or teams.
    - **Projects** — browse and open all active projects within your current workspace.
    - **Inbox** — view messages, comments, and activity directed at you.
    - **Reports** — access analytics dashboards and exportable reports.
    - **Integrations** — manage connected third-party tools and services.
    - **Trash** — recover recently deleted items before they're permanently removed.

    Click the **collapse icon** (double arrow) at the bottom of the sidebar to minimise it to icon-only view and reclaim horizontal space. Click any icon to expand it again.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Top Bar">
    The top bar spans the full width of the screen and contains global controls that are available no matter where you are in the app.

    **Top bar elements from left to right:**

    - **Workspace switcher** — displays your current workspace name. Click it to switch to another workspace or create a new one.
    - **Global search** — click the search icon or press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` to search across all projects, items, and team members.
    - **Notifications bell** — shows a badge when you have unread notifications. Click it to open the notifications panel.
    - **Quick actions (+)** — create a new project, task, or record without leaving your current view.
    - **Help & Support** — access documentation, release notes, and contact support.
    - **Profile avatar** — click your avatar to open account settings, switch themes, or log out.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Main Panel">
    The main panel occupies the central and largest area of the dashboard. Its content updates dynamically based on what you've selected in the sidebar.

    **Default main panel sections:**

    - **Overview cards** — at-a-glance summaries of recent activity, upcoming deadlines, and team updates.
    - **Recent items** — a list of the projects and records you've accessed most recently, so you can pick up where you left off.
    - **Activity feed** — a chronological stream of changes made by you and your teammates across shared workspaces.
    - **Pinned items** — any projects or views you've marked as favourites appear here for fast access.

    Use the **layout toggle** in the top-right corner of the main panel to switch between list, grid, and board views. Your preference is saved automatically per section.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Settings">
    The settings panel gives you full control over your account preferences, workspace configuration, and team management. Access it by clicking your **profile avatar** in the top bar and selecting **Settings**, or by navigating to **Sidebar → Settings**.

    **Settings is divided into the following sections:**

    - **Profile** — update your display name, avatar, timezone, and language.
    - **Account** — manage your email address, password, and two-factor authentication.
    - **Workspace** — configure workspace name, branding, member roles, and permissions.
    - **Billing** — view your current plan, manage payment methods, and download invoices.
    - **Notifications** — customise which events trigger in-app alerts and email digests.
    - **Integrations** — connect, disconnect, and configure third-party apps and APIs.
    - **Security** — review active sessions, manage API keys, and configure SSO settings.

    Changes you make in Settings take effect immediately unless a confirmation step is noted on the page.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Key Dashboard Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Search" icon="magnifying-glass">
    The global search bar lets you find any project, record, team member, or file across your entire account in seconds. Press `Cmd + K` (Mac) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows/Linux) from anywhere to open it instantly. Use filters to narrow results by type, date, or workspace.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Notifications" icon="bell">
    The notifications panel keeps you informed about mentions, assignments, comments, and status changes. Click the bell icon in the top bar to view all recent alerts. You can mark individual notifications as read, or clear them all at once. Adjust your notification preferences under **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Quick Actions" icon="bolt">
    The quick actions button (the **+** icon in the top bar) lets you create new projects, tasks, records, or invitations without navigating away from your current page. Each option opens a lightweight modal so you can capture information fast and get back to what you were doing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Customize View" icon="sliders">
    Tailor your dashboard layout to match the way you work. Use the **layout toggle** in the main panel to switch between list, grid, and board views. Pin your most-used projects to the sidebar for one-click access, and reorder sidebar items by dragging them to your preferred position.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Use keyboard shortcuts to navigate the dashboard without touching your mouse. Press `?` at any time to open the keyboard shortcuts reference overlay, which lists every available shortcut for your current view.
</Tip>
